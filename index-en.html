Now in testing with friends and family

Everyone takes photos. You see them all later.

Flash is a shared disposable camera for your birthday, trip or party. Everyone adds photos, quotes and messages, and everything stays sealed until the day the event opens.

See an exampleHow it works

How it works

  1. Create an event

    Give it a name and choose the day it opens: tomorrow, in a month or in a year.

  2. Invite your friends

    Share a code. Participants add photos, quotes and messages; viewers only see the end result.

  3. Open it together

    On the opening day everyone gets a notification and you finally see everything the others captured.

Why Flash is different

Sealed until the opening day

You only see what you add yourself. The rest stays a surprise, just like a roll of film that still has to be developed.

Only your group

An event has at most 15 people. Nobody else can find or view it.

More than photos

Also capture what was said, or write a message to your future selves.

Straight to your photo library

When the event opens, Flash puts all the photos in an album on your phone.
