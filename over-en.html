Hi, I'm Thom

I study Building Engineering, and Flash is my own project alongside my studies.

How Flash started

After every birthday, holiday or party it's the same story: everyone has photos, but they never end up in one place. They stay scattered across phones and group chats, and after a week nobody looks at them anymore.

I wanted to build an app myself and chose the idea I liked most: a shared memory capsule. Fill it together, open it later. That's how Flash was born.
