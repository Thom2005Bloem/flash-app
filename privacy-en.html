Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. The Dutch version is leading in case of differences.

This privacy policy explains what data the Flash app collects, why, and what your rights are. Flash is made by Thom Bloem, who is responsible for processing your data. We don't sell data, show no ads and don't track you outside the app.

What data we collect

DataPurposeHow long
Email address and passwordTo create an account and log in. Your password is stored encrypted; nobody can read it.Until you delete your account
Your nameSo other members of an event can see who added something.Until you delete your account
Events and membershipsThe name, start and opening date of events and who is a member, with which role (creator, participant or viewer).Until the event is deleted or you leave it
Photos, videos, quotes and messagesThe memories you add to an event.Until the memory, the event or your account is deleted
Reactions (emojis)Showing how members react to a memory after the event has opened.Until you remove the reaction, or the memory or your account is deleted
Reports of inappropriate contentIf you report a memory, we store which one and why, so we can review it.Up to 12 months

Flash does not collect location, contacts or advertising data. There are no ads or analytics services in the app.

Who can see your memories

While an event is sealed, nobody else sees what you added. You can see your own quotes and messages; your own photos and videos only during the first 5 minutes, so you can still undo them. Once the event opens, all members of that event can see your photos, videos, quotes and messages, react to them and save them in their own phone's library.

Access on your phone

  • Camera and microphone: only when you take a photo or video in Flash, or scan a QR code to join. Sound is only recorded with videos.
  • Photo library: to choose a photo or video you add and, if you choose to, to put the photos and videos of an opened event in an album. You decide this per event. We don't read any other photos.
  • Editing on your phone: the film filter and resizing of photos happen on your own phone, before anything is uploaded.
  • Notifications: reminders during an event, the notification that an event opens and a look-back after one year. These are scheduled on your phone itself.

You can turn off this access at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, a database and file storage service, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (within the European Union). Supabase only processes the data on behalf of Flash. Data travels encrypted over the internet.

How long we keep data

  • Your data is kept as long as your account exists.
  • If you delete a memory, or the creator deletes an event, it disappears from the app immediately. The related photo and video files are removed from storage within 30 days.
  • If you delete your account (in the app, via your profile), your profile, your memories and the events you created are deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to its use. You can delete your account directly in the app. For other requests, email flashapp.support05@gmail.com; we respond within one month. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Age

Flash is intended for people aged 16 and older.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, we update the date at the top. For major changes we will let you know in the app.
