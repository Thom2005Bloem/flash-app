Vision

Why Flash exists and where it is heading.

The best moments are the ones you share. Flash makes sure you look back on them together too.

What Flash stands for

Live it first

Because you only see the photos later, you don't spend the whole evening on your phone checking what others are posting. You enjoy the moment and look back later.

Remember together

After a weekend away, the photos are scattered across ten phones. Flash brings them together, along with what was said.

The surprise is back

You used to wait days for your film to be developed. Flash brings that feeling back: the opening day is a small celebration in itself.

Private and fair

No ads, no followers, no public profiles. Your memories are only for the people who were there. Guests always join for free.

Where Flash is now

  1. The app is built

    Events, invite codes, photos, quotes, messages and the opening day all work.

  2. Now: testing with friends and family

    A small group uses Flash at real events and gives feedback.

  3. Improving based on feedback

    Whatever testers miss or find difficult gets fixed first.

  4. Launch in the App Store

    After that, anyone with an iPhone can download Flash.
