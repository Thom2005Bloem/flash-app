Support

Need help with Flash? Here you'll find answers to the most frequently asked questions.

How do I join an event?

Ask the creator for a code. In Flash, tap join and enter the code. With a participant code you can add memories; with a viewer code you only see the end result.

Why can't I see other people's photos?

The event is still sealed. You only see what you added yourself. On the opening day you'll see everything.

I forgot my password

Email the address below from your account's email address. You'll then get a link to choose a new password.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, via your profile. Your account, your memories and the events you created will then be deleted. Read more in the privacy policy.

Contact

Email flashapp.support05@gmail.com. You'll usually get a reply within two days.
